Make yourself at home

  1. Find the bumps on F and J.

    Both keys have a small bump. Use them to orient your hands without looking at the keyboard.

  2. Rest on A S D F · J K L ;

    Keep your fingers relaxed on the home row. These are your starting positions.

  3. Let your thumbs handle Space.

    Use either thumb and keep your other fingers near the home row.

Posture & typing tips

Sit upright with feet supported, elbows relaxed, and wrists neutral. Keep a light touch and take regular breaks.

Use the opposite hand’s Shift key for capitals and symbols. Press Enter for ↵ and Space for ·. In code, Tab enters four spaces.

The guide suggests fingers; it cannot detect which finger you use. Use a physical US QWERTY keyboard for two-hand practice.
